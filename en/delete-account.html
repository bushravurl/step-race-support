Delete account

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Delete in the app

Open Profile → Settings → Delete my account. Enter your password and give final confirmation. Successful deletion closes your sessions. Deletion is permanent and removes coins and earned cosmetics too.

If you cannot access the app

From your registered email address, email steprace.support@gmail.com with the subject “Step Race account deletion request”. State that you want to delete your Step Race account and related personal data; you may include your username. Do not send a password or identity document. The email link opens your mail app; you must send the message yourself. It does not trigger automatic deletion. Support processes the request after verifying account ownership.

What is deleted and retained

Account/profile, sessions, step records, friendships, inventory, achievements and personal notification records are removed from the active database. Shared race results remain as “Deleted user” with the personal account link removed to preserve other users’ history. Security/rate-limit records, support correspondence and hosting logs may have a separate scope; see Privacy. Uninstalling the app or revoking health permission does not delete your account.
