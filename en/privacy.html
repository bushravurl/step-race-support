STEP RACE

Privacy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

1. Scope and contact

This notice explains data use in the Step Race mobile app and this support website. The developer is Büşra Vural. For privacy and data requests, contact steprace.support@gmail.com.

2. Account and profile information

When you register, you provide an email address, username, display name and password. The server stores a password hash. Profile preferences, selected character/outfit/marker, level, XP, coins, inventory, achievements, friendships, invitations and race participation are processed to provide app features.

3. Steps and activity data

With your permission, we read daily step totals from Apple Health/HealthKit on iOS or Health Connect on supported Android devices. Daily step totals, local date, source/device information and sync timestamps are sent to our server and linked to your account. They support race progress, rankings, statistics and rewards. Distance/calorie figures shown in the app are estimates, not medical measurements. This feature does not request heart rate, diagnoses, medical records or GPS location.

4. Permissions and visibility

You can revoke read access in your device’s Apple Health or Health Connect settings. Revoking permission stops new readings; it does not automatically erase previously synced records. Your profile name, character, race steps, progress and rank are visible to other participants in shared races. Friend-profile step/achievement sharing settings are applied separately and do not hide shared race results.

5. Technical and support data

Session/refresh records support secure sign-in, and login-attempt records help prevent abuse. Device platform and push addressing tokens may be processed when push delivery is enabled. Hosting/server providers may retain connection and security logs. When you email support, your address, message and supplied attachments are used to handle your request.

6. Service providers and sharing

Our server is hosted on Render and our database uses MongoDB Atlas. Support correspondence uses Gmail. Enabled remote notifications may pass through the platform’s notification infrastructure. Processing may occur outside Türkiye depending on server regions and provider infrastructure. Providers apply their own privacy terms to their services. Health data is not used for targeted advertising, marketing or data sales. Necessary information may be disclosed in response to lawful requests from competent authorities.

7. Retention and deletion

While your account is active, account, progress and synced step records are retained to provide the service. In-app account deletion removes your account and related session, step, friendship, inventory, achievement, notification and progress records from the active database. To preserve other participants’ results, shared race results remain as “Deleted user” with the personal account link removed; titles/descriptions of races you created are cleared. Security/rate-limit records, support correspondence and provider logs may not be automatically removed by the same account deletion operation. Contact support about their retention/deletion and any backups.

8. Requests and security

Email support to request information, correction or deletion of account data. We may verify account ownership before disclosing or deleting data; we do not ask for your password by email. We use measures such as HTTPS and secure session storage, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

9. This website and changes

This website contains no advertising, analytics or third-party tracking scripts. A language preference may be saved in your browser. GitHub Pages may process connection information to serve and secure the site under its own privacy terms. If our data use changes, we will update this notice and its date.
