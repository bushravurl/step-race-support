Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Registration and your choices

App: Step Race: Adım Yarışı (Step Race). Developer: Busra Vural. You can open this policy on the registration screen and actively select the initially unchecked agreement box. You can choose not to complete registration without agreeing. This agreement does not grant health-data or notification permissions; these are requested separately on your device and can be revoked in the relevant settings.

1. Scope and contact

This notice explains data use in the Step Race mobile app and this support website. The developer is Busra Vural. For privacy and data requests, contact steprace.support@gmail.com.

2. Account and profile information

When you register, you provide an email address, username, display name and password. The server stores a password hash. Profile preferences, selected character/outfit/marker, level, XP, coins, inventory, achievements, friendships, invitations and race participation are processed to provide app features.

3. Steps and activity data

With your permission, we read daily step totals from Apple Health/HealthKit on iOS or Health Connect on supported Android devices. Daily step totals, local date, source/device information and sync timestamps are sent to our server and linked to your account. They support race progress, rankings, statistics and rewards. On supported devices, the phone step sensor may also be used with your permission. The app does not provide medical measurements or diagnosis. This feature does not request heart rate, diagnoses, medical records or GPS location.

On supported versions and devices, with the required permissions, the app may also read and sync step data with our server while it is not in the foreground. Background access supports updates to daily goals and race progress; its frequency depends on operating-system, battery and network conditions and is not guaranteed to be continuous or real time. We do not write health data to Apple Health or Health Connect.

On Android, Health Connect takes priority. When Health Connect is unavailable, you may separately consent to phone-sensor tracking on eligible devices. This option does not read Huawei Health history or smartwatch data; it counts steps detected by the phone after tracking starts. Tracking displays an ongoing notification and can be stopped from that notification. Daily totals from different sources for the same day are not added together; the server uses the previously recorded daily total and only new increases.

4. Permissions and visibility

You can revoke read access in your device’s Apple Health or Health Connect settings. Revoking permission stops new readings; it does not automatically erase previously synced records. Your profile name, character, race steps, progress and rank are visible to other participants in shared races. Friend-profile step/achievement sharing settings are applied separately and do not hide shared race results.

Password reset emails

If you forget your password, we use Brevo’s transactional email service to send a single-use verification code to your registered email address. Brevo processes the recipient email address, code and email content for this purpose; step or health data is not included in these emails. Codes expire after 15 minutes; requesting a new code invalidates the previous code. Time-limited verification records and attempt counts support validation and abuse prevention. Code content awaiting delivery is encrypted on our server; the encrypted queue payload is removed after successful delivery. A successful password reset invalidates your old sessions. You can ignore a reset email you did not request.

5. Technical and support data

Session/refresh records support secure sign-in, and login-attempt records help prevent abuse. Device platform and push addressing tokens may be processed when push delivery is enabled. Hosting/server providers may retain connection and security logs. When you email support, your address, message and supplied attachments are used to handle your request.

6. Service providers and sharing

Our server is hosted on Render and our database uses MongoDB Atlas. Support correspondence uses Gmail. Password reset emails are delivered through Brevo. Enabled remote notifications may pass through the platform’s notification infrastructure. Processing may occur outside Türkiye depending on server regions and provider infrastructure. Providers apply their own privacy terms to their services. Health data is not used for targeted advertising, marketing or data sales. Necessary information may be disclosed in response to lawful requests from competent authorities.

7. Retention and deletion

While your account is active, account, progress and synced step records are retained to provide the service. In-app account deletion removes your account and related session, step, friendship, inventory, achievement, notification and progress records from the active database. To preserve other participants’ results, shared race results remain as “Deleted user” with the personal account link removed; titles/descriptions of races you created are cleared. Security/rate-limit records, support correspondence and provider logs may not be automatically removed by the same account deletion operation. Contact support about their retention/deletion and any backups.

8. Requests and security

Email support to request information, correction or deletion of account data. We may verify account ownership before disclosing or deleting data; we do not ask for your password by email. We use measures such as HTTPS and secure session storage, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

Share cards and artwork

Your share card contains the selected period’s steps, display name, username and rank. The top-three template contains the relevant participants’ display names and step totals. Cards are generated on your device. Share opens the device share sheet, where you choose the destination app. Save lets you choose a file location. Exported copies are subject to the destination app or storage provider’s terms. App artwork such as characters and themes may be downloaded through Cloudflare and cached on your device.

9. This website and changes

This website contains no advertising, analytics or third-party tracking scripts. A language preference may be saved in your browser. GitHub Pages may process connection information to serve and secure the site under its own privacy terms. If our data use changes, we will update this notice and its date.
