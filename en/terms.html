Terms of use

Last updated: 15 October 2026

1. Service

Step Race helps you compete with friends using step data, track progress and earn virtual cosmetics. It does not provide medical services or medical advice. Choose activity goals appropriate to your own circumstances.

2. Account responsibility

Keep your account information accurate and current, and protect your password. Do not access another person’s account without permission or impersonate others. Contact support if you discover an account security issue.

3. Races and conduct

Do not submit fake/manipulated step data, cheat, harass others, access systems without permission or disrupt the service. Do not include others’ private information in race names or descriptions. Results may be delayed or corrected depending on devices, health providers, permissions and sync timing.

4. Coins and virtual items

In this release, coins are earned through app activities and used for virtual items. Coins, outfits and head markers are not money, cannot be redeemed for cash and cannot be transferred outside the app. This release does not offer real-money purchases or subscriptions. Character-specific items may require unlocking the relevant character.

5. Service operation

Features may be temporarily unavailable for maintenance, technical issues or abuse prevention. To protect security and race fairness, we may investigate violations, correct invalid records and apply necessary account restrictions. Contact support to request a review.

6. Closing an account and contact

You can close your account through in-app deletion or the web account deletion route. The Privacy and Delete account pages explain the consequences. These terms do not limit mandatory legal rights. Questions: steprace.support@gmail.com. We update these terms when significant service changes require it.
