Frequently asked questions

Contact support

Email Step Race support at steprace.support@gmail.com. Include a short description, phone model, iOS/Android version, app version and steps to reproduce the problem. Do not send passwords, verification codes or unnecessary health information.

Steps are not appearing

Check your step records in Apple Health or Health Connect and the read permission granted to Step Race. Reopen the app and sync again. Provider availability depends on your device; Huawei Health integration is not offered in this release.

My purchased marker is missing from a race

Go to Profile → Collection → Your head markers and select Equip. One head marker can be equipped at a time. This release uses coins earned in the app; it does not offer real-money purchases.

I want to delete my account

Use Profile → Settings → Delete my account, or follow the email request route on our account deletion page. Uninstalling the app does not delete your account.
